Privacy Policy – Bean's PokéBuilder

Last updated: November 2026

Bean's PokéBuilder is a free hobby site for building Pokémon teams. It has no ads, no tracking and no accounts of its own.

What is stored

Your teams, calculator teams, trainer progress and profile (display name, avatar, favorites and top 6) are saved in your own browser on your own device.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the site asks for one permission: access to a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It uses that folder to keep one file with the data listed above so it can sync between your devices. It cannot see your other Drive files.

The site does not receive or store your password, email address or any other Google account details. It does not send your data to me or to any third party. You can stop syncing by signing out in the app, and you can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Built to collect as little as possible

This site was designed so that it never stores emails or passwords. There is no account system and no server holding your information. Your data stays in your own browser and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive, where you control it.

You are in control

  • Sign out in the app at any time to stop syncing. Your data stays on that device.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the synced file from your Drive using Google Drive's settings (Manage apps, then delete hidden app data).
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove everything saved on that device.
